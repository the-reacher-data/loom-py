---
name: severity-rubric
description: Classify an incident as sev1, sev2 or sev3 from its blast radius and duration.
---

Assign sev1 when a customer-facing surface is unavailable or data is at risk,
sev2 when a surface is degraded but usable, and sev3 for everything else. State
the blast radius and the elapsed duration you based the classification on, and
say when the evidence does not support any level yet.
